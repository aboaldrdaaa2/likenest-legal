Like Nest - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

Like Nest ("the app") is operated by Omar Drweesh ("we"). Contact: aboaldrdaaa2@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: when you sign in with Google we receive your email address and a Google account ID through Firebase Authentication. We never receive your password.
  • Profile data: the nickname you choose, your points balance, daily reward progress, rewarded video counters (time of the last video and number of videos per day) and the time you accepted the Terms of use.
  • Activity data: tasks you create (link, type, reward, remaining amount), tasks you complete, and timestamps.
  • Leaderboard data: your nickname and the number of tasks you completed in the current week, visible to other signed-in users.
  • Community content: chat messages you send, shown with your nickname and time, and reports you submit about other users.
  • Advertising data: the app shows rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions to serve and measure ads, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • On your device only: the list of users you chose to hide.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts or photos.

3. How we use data

To sign you in, keep your points and tasks, run the community chat and weekly leaderboard, show ads and limit reward abuse, prevent fraud, review reports, and enforce the Terms of use.

4. Who can see what

Your nickname, chat messages and weekly task count are visible to other signed-in users. Task links you create are visible to other signed-in users. Your email address and balance are not shown to other users.

5. Services we use and your ad choices

Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), Google Sign-In and Google AdMob process data on our behalf or as independent providers under Google's terms and privacy policy. Data is stored on Google servers, which may be located outside your country. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app shows a consent form when it opens and does not request ads until you have made your choice. You can change your choice at any time in Profile > Ad privacy settings.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the service providers above, or when required by law or to protect users and the app.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account inside the app (Profile > Delete account) or request deletion without installing the app on our account deletion page.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict some processing, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, email aboaldrdaaa2@gmail.com.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by access rules. No system is completely secure.

10. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Third-party platforms

Tasks open links on other platforms such as Facebook. Those platforms have their own policies. Like Nest is not affiliated with Facebook or Meta.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.

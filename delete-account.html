Delete your Like Nest account

App: Like Nest (abbadeh.apps.likenest) - Developer: Omar Drweesh

Option 1: inside the app

  1. Stop your running tasks first (My tasks) so your remaining points are refunded.
  2. Open Profile > Delete account.
  3. Confirm with the same Google account.

Option 2: without the app

Send an email to aboaldrdaaa2@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Like Nest account, from the Google email address you used to sign in, and include your nickname. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We complete requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in record and the email address linked to it.
  • Your profile: nickname, points balance, daily reward and video counters, and the time you accepted the Terms.
  • Your weekly leaderboard entry.

What may be kept

For safety and fraud prevention we may keep chat messages you posted, records of tasks you created or completed, and reports you submitted or that were made about you. If you want these deleted as well, say so in your request and we will delete them within 30 days.

Data held by Google AdMob is controlled by Google. You can manage it in your Google account settings.

See also our Privacy Policy.
